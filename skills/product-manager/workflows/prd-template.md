## 1. Executive Summary

Brief overview (2-3 sentences) explaining:
- What problem this solves
- Who it's for
- Key business value

## 2. Problem Statement

### Problem Description
Clear description of the problem users face.

### User Impact
- Who experiences this problem
- Frequency of occurrence
- Current workaround (if any)

### Business Impact
- Revenue impact
- Customer satisfaction impact
- Strategic alignment
